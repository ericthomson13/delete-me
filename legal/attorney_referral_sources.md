# Attorney Referral Sources

`delete-me` does **not** endorse, recommend, or refer users to any specific
attorney or law firm. Doing so would create unauthorized-practice-of-law (UPL)
exposure for the project and is the wrong job for a software tool.

When the compliance audit detects that a broker has not honored a deletion
request, the generated evidence package includes pointers to the following
**neutral, jurisdiction-agnostic directories** where a consumer can locate a
plaintiff-side privacy attorney if they choose to escalate further:

## Primary directory

- **National Association of Consumer Advocates (NACA)** —
  https://www.consumeradvocates.org/find-an-attorney
  NACA is the leading membership organization for plaintiff-side consumer
  attorneys in the United States. The "Find an Attorney" directory is
  searchable by state and by practice area (data privacy, identity theft,
  consumer reporting).

## State bar lookups (use the consumer's state)

- **California State Bar** — https://www.calbar.ca.gov/Public/Need-Legal-Help
- **New York State Bar Association — Lawyer Referral & Information Service** —
  https://nysba.org/lawyer-referral-service/
- **Texas State Bar — Lawyer Referral Service** — https://www.texasbar.com/AM/Template.cfm?Section=Find_A_Lawyer
- For other states, use the appropriate state bar's lawyer-referral service.

## Advocacy & complaint avenues (not legal representation)

These organizations do not provide legal counsel to individuals, but they
track data-broker noncompliance and accept consumer complaints that may inform
their enforcement or policy work:

- **California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA)** — https://cppa.ca.gov/contact/
  (CPPA enforces the Delete Act and CCPA.)
- **California Attorney General — Consumer Complaint** —
  https://oag.ca.gov/contact/consumer-complaint-against-business-or-company
- **Federal Trade Commission — Consumer Complaint Assistant** —
  https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/
- **Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC)** — https://epic.org/
- **Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF)** — https://www.eff.org/issues/privacy
- **Consumer Reports — Permission Slip** — https://innovation.consumerreports.org/initiative/permission-slip/

## Why no firm list

A static curated list of plaintiff firms would (a) become stale quickly,
(b) create an implied endorsement we are not qualified to make, and
(c) carry referral-fee implications under various state bar rules. NACA and
state bar directories are the right channel.
